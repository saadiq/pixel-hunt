Crumb Hunt

  1. Tap a lane to send its top squad to a holding slot.
  2. Ants leave the nest, run the track around the board, and tunnel through eaten gaps to reach blocks of their color from any side.
  3. A squad leaves when its ants are fed. If every slot is full and nobody can reach food, you're stuck.
  4. Every deal has at least one winning order. Use undo or a hint when you're boxed in.
